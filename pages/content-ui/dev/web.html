Chipmakers rally as NVIDIA guides higher; Tesla and Palantir follow

NVIDIA (NASDAQ: NVDA) rose 4% after the bell on stronger data-centre guidance, and the rest of the Magnificent Seven moved with it: Microsoft, Apple, Alphabet, Amazon and Meta Platforms all finished higher, while Tesla added 2.1% ahead of its delivery numbers.

On-chain, the tokenized versions tracked the move. NVDAB on BNB Chain and NVDAon on Solana both repriced within minutes, and traders holding $TSLA, $PLTR and $COIN watched the tape into the close. Coinbase and Robinhood rose with bitcoin.

Not every capitalised word is a company. TURN ON THE LIGHTS is a sentence, not onsemi (ON); the word NOW in a headline is not ServiceNow (NYSE: NOW); and a Circle on a diagram is not Circle Internet Group.

Pre-IPO names were busy too: OpenAI, Anthropic and SpaceX tokens on Solana all traded above their last marks, while Micron and SanDisk dragged memory stocks lower.
